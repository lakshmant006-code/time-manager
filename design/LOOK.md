# LOOK — Summer motion design video

## Palette
- Background: `#05070f` (near-black navy — the shader's resting dark tone)
- Neutral 1: `#ffffff` (white — type, logo, UI chrome)
- Neutral 2: `#aab3e8` (soft periwinkle — the shader's light blob tone, used for secondary glows/strokes)
- Accent: `#1900ff` (brand blue — the one thing to look at: timer digits, highlighted states, the CTA)

## Type
- Headline: **Avant Gothic** (brand display font, weight 600)
- Body / caption: **Inter**
- Scale (1.25, three steps): caption **46px** → secondary **58px** → headline **72px**
- Headline (72px) is 6.7% of the 1080px frame height — clears the 5% minimum.

## Grid (1920×1080)
- Title-safe: 96px sides, 54px top/bottom
- Action-safe: 67px sides, 38px top/bottom

## Shape language
- Rounded, dimensional, glass — matches the live product (14–30px corner radii, translucent frosted panels, 1.5px light strokes at ~70% opacity).
- Soft blurred color-blobs (shader-style) rather than hard gradients or flat fields.
- A faint grain/noise layer over backgrounds, same as the app's own shader.
- The screen never goes flat/blank between beats — the shader-void background or a UI surface is always present.

## Motion carriers (for later gates)
- Beat 1→2: scattered fragments pull inward and dissolve into the shader.
- Beat 2→3: the logo mark cross-dissolves into the login card, which clears to reveal the dashboard.
- Beat 3→4: a stat-card row carries into a close-up (camera push, not a cut).
- Beat 4→5: the running timer's highlight bar slides into the next screen.
- Beat 5→6: dashboard dissolves back into the shader-void for the lockup.
